# BUG-003 — S50 Pro Telescope Identity Recognition

**Classification:** Product defect — S50 Pro compatibility
**Status:** STARTED

## Objective

Correct the identity-recognition limitation that permits cross-telescope light
association during archive reconstruction.

## Confirmed metadata and root cause

| Telescope | TELESCOP | INSTRUME |
|---|---|---|
| S50 | `S50_99643794` | `Seestar S50` |
| S50 Pro | `S50 Pro_edfe7fb2` | Absent |

Existing recognition accepts only `S50_[0-9a-f]{8}`. S50 Pro identities are
therefore treated as unknown, and the existing compatibility rules permit unknown
identities to associate with recognised identities.

## Required behaviour

Recognise complete telescope identities matching:

- `S50_[0-9a-f]{8}`
- `S50 Pro_[0-9a-f]{8}`

Matching must be case-insensitive. The telescope model must remain part of the
identity. Different recognised telescope identities must never cross-associate
lights and stacks.

Preserve existing behaviour for missing, malformed, and unrecognised identities.

## Validation requirements

Automated tests must cover S50 and S50 Pro recognition, case variations, complete
identity matching, the same hexadecimal suffix across different models, different
recognised devices, malformed and unsupported identities, unknown-identity
compatibility, and overlapping S50/S50 Pro observations.

Original dataset validation must establish six complete observations, three per
telescope, no mixed telescope membership, no incorrectly stack-only S50
observations, and preservation of source data.
